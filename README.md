## 🧠 Research goal

Test whether AI video models can maintain coherent object physics during continuous interaction between a human hand and a moving basketball.

Focus areas:

- ball trajectory stability  
- hand–ball contact consistency  
- motion continuity  
- gravity behaviour  
- temporal coherence  

## ⚙️ Method

Single-shot generation.  
Continuous dribble action.  
No cuts.  

Prompt emphasis:

- realistic basketball motion  
- continuous dribbling  
- consistent ball size  
- stable trajectory  
- natural gravity  

## 🔍 Observations

Common model failures:

- ball deformation  
- trajectory drift  
- contact loss  
- size fluctuation  
- temporal jitter  

## ✅ Successful cases

Two generations showed stable dribble physics:

- consistent ball trajectory  
- preserved contact rhythm  
- minimal deformation  
- coherent motion continuity  

## 📌 Insight

AI video models often struggle with persistent object identity during repeated contact motion.

Explicit physical constraints in prompts improve stability of ball behaviour and interaction coherence.
